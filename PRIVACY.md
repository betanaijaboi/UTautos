# Privacy Policy — UT Autos

Last updated: 2026-08-25

## What we collect

- Account info: name, email, phone (via Supabase Auth).
- Booking details: vehicle/aircraft info, service address, appointment
  times.
- Payment info: handled directly by Stripe/Paystack/PayPal/Apple Pay —
  UT Autos does not store your card or bank details itself.

## Why we collect it

To create your account, process bookings, dispatch detailers, and handle
payments.

## Who we share it with

- **Supabase** — database, auth, and row-level security provider.
- **Payment processors** (Stripe, Paystack, PayPal) — only the data needed
  to process your payment; see their respective privacy policies.
- **Assigned detailer** — your service address and vehicle/aircraft
  details, so they can perform the booked service.
- We do not sell your personal data.

## Your rights (Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023)

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your data at any
time by contacting us below. Some booking/payment records may be retained
as required for tax or dispute-resolution purposes even after a deletion
request.

## Security

Data is stored in Supabase with row-level security; payments are handled
by PCI-compliant third-party processors, not stored on our servers.
